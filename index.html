Questions we hear first

Do I need to change software?

No. We work with Xero, MYOB, and QuickBooks. If your records are a shoebox, we pick one together and migrate you properly.

How long does handover take?

About a week. We map your accounts, chase stray statements, and reconcile one full month before anything else changes.

Who actually does the work?

A named bookkeeper from our small team, not a ticket queue. You get their direct email and number on day one.

Are you registered BAS agents?

Yes. Every BAS we lodge goes through a registered agent, which automatically extends your lodgment and payment deadlines.
